We analyzed the operator usage of DBPile queries and compared them to the TPC-H and TPC-DS benchmarks. As depicted in 
\cref{tab:op-usage}, DBPile queries use far fewer logical operators than the TPC benchmarks.
This comes from the fact that a lot of the queries in DBPile are simple \texttt{SELECT} statements from a transactional workload,
while the TPC benchmarks resemble an analytical workload with complex joins and aggregations.

We analyzed the distribution of logical column types used in DBPile, which are shown in \cref{tab:column-types}, which is
